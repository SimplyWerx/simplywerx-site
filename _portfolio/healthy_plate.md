An interactive, tactile experience that helps children discover the basics of balanced nutrition. Through play and exploration, kids learn how to build a healthy plate, understand food groups, and make smarter eating choices in a fun, memorable way.

<iframe width="100%" height="600" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/mUi0ZnHfQtk" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-fullscreen" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Custom‑fabricated touch sensors are synchronized with the spoons, ensuring they respond exclusively to spoon contact. Leveraging advanced electric‑field sensing techniques, the system achieves robust performance with consistently low error rates.

These custom sensors can be adapted to turn virtually any object into a touch target, programmed to recognize only specific actuators — in this case, the spoons — for precise and reliable interaction.
