Kids learn how to “complete the circuit” by inserting the correct cable into the matching socket. When the circuit is completed, flashing lights activate to “keep away the lions,” making the challenge both fun and educational

<img src="assets/img/optimized-large/IMG-20251009-WA0002.jpg" alt="Custom fabricated socket and plug to house electromagnet and RFID" class="img-fluid w-75">

The system combines electromagnets, customized RFID antennas, and embedded RFID tags. Inserting the correct cable energizes an electromagnet at the socket. After a timed interval, the electromagnets automatically switch off, causing the cables to retract back into their holders — resetting the puzzle for the next participant.
