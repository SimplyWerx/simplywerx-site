Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

We at SimplyWerx are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with Compass Steel Pro (the "App").

We collect the absolute minimum data needed for the App to function. We do not collect, store, or share personal information that directly identifies you (such as name, email, contacts, or files).

1. Data Processed on Device

The following data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Device sensor data (magnetometer, accelerometer, gyroscope) used for compass orientation and heading.
  • Optional location data used only for on-device calculations (e.g., true north, magnetic declination, sunrise/sunset, altitude). No location data is collected, stored, or transmitted.

2. Your Consent

By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
